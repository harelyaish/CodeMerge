FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work on Apple Silicon and Intel?

Yes — one universal build covers both, on macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.

My Mac has no notch. Does it work?

Yes. On Macs without a notch, the panel drops from the top-center of the screen the same way.

Why does macOS say it's from an "unidentified developer"?

CodeMerge isn't distributed through the App Store or Apple's notarization, so a browser download gets flagged like any indie app. The Terminal install command skips that entirely, or right-click the app → Open once.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Weather, Stocks prices and the currency converter make plain HTTPS calls with no account or key, and the video downloader talks only to the site you give it. Clipboard history, screenshots, notes, medicines and settings stay on your Mac.

Can I use my license on more than one Mac?

The license is personal and non-transferable, but nothing technical stops you using it on your own Macs — it isn't hardware-locked.

What if I want a refund?

Refunds are handled by Gumroad from your purchase receipt email.

How do updates work?

Settings → General → Check for Updates. Updates are signed, so a download is verified before it replaces the app.
