Get started

Installed in under a minute.

Recommended — skips the "unidentified developer" step.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harelyaish/CodeMerge/main/install.sh | bash

Two commands:

brew tap harelyaish/codemerge https://github.com/harelyaish/CodeMerge brew install --cask codemerge

Download the zip, unzip, and drag CodeMerge.app to Applications. macOS will block the first launch — see the steps on the right.

macOS won't open it?

Normal for any indie app outside the App Store. One-time fix:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open, then click Open.
  2. If it only offers "Move to Trash": System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click Open Anyway.
  3. Approve once — every later launch is normal.
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work on Apple Silicon and Intel?

Yes — one universal build covers both, on macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.

Why does macOS say it's from an "unidentified developer"?

CodeMerge isn't distributed through the App Store or Apple's notarization, so a browser download gets flagged like any indie app. The Terminal install command skips that entirely, or right-click the app → Open once.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Only Weather and the currency converter make plain HTTPS calls, with no account or key. Clipboard history, screenshots, notes and settings stay on your Mac.

Can I use my license on more than one Mac?

The license is personal and non-transferable, but nothing technical stops you using it on your own Macs — it isn't hardware-locked.

What if I want a refund?

Refunds are handled by Gumroad from your purchase receipt email.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Not yet — it's coming in a future update: sign in with Google and see your meetings in CodeMerge, with a countdown and a one-tap Join. Until then, calendars added to the Mac's own Calendar app already work.

How do updates work?

Settings → General → Check for Updates. Updates are signed, so a download is verified before it replaces the app.
